# Photography and Editorial Prompts

## 1. Natural editorial portrait

**Best for:** profiles and features · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a photorealistic 4:5 editorial portrait of {{subject_description}} in {{environment}}, engaged in
{{natural_action}}. Camera: {{lens_behavior}}, eye-level, realistic working distance. Light: {{motivated_light}}.
Preserve real skin texture, fabric wear, flyaway hair, and subtle asymmetry. Color grade: {{grade}}.

No beauty-filter skin, impossible bokeh, fashion retouching, duplicated jewelry, distorted hands, text, logos,
or stereotyped props. The moment should feel observed rather than staged.
```

## 2. Food editorial hero

**Best for:** restaurant and recipe stories · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 3:2 editorial food photograph of {{dish}} just after {{freshness_moment}}. Show authentic portion size,
ingredient texture, steam or condensation only where physically plausible, and tableware appropriate to
{{setting}}. Camera: {{angle}}; light: soft directional window light; palette: {{palette}}.

No plastic-looking food, excessive garnish, floating ingredients, fake steam, brand marks, hands unless requested,
or unrelated table clutter. Maintain natural edible color and credible shadows.
```

## 3. Architecture feature image

**Best for:** design publication · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a photorealistic 16:9 architectural image of {{building_or_room}} emphasizing {{design_intent}}.
Viewpoint: {{viewpoint}} with straight verticals and credible wide-angle perspective. Time: {{time}}.
Materials must show accurate roughness, reflection, joinery, and scale. Include {{human_presence}} only as a scale cue.

No warped walls, impossible cantilevers, repeated furniture, mixed light directions, oversized rooms, fake signage,
or decorative additions not in the brief. Keep circulation and structure plausible.
```

## 4. Travel sense-of-place photograph

**Best for:** destination editorial · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a candid 3:2 travel photograph in {{specific_place}} during {{time_or_weather}}. Focus on {{everyday_subject}}
rather than a postcard landmark. Show locally plausible architecture, clothing, transport, vegetation, and street
details. Camera: {{camera}}; color: natural; mood: {{mood}}.

If grounding is available, verify distinctive place details. Avoid exoticizing people, staged costumes, invented
signage, mixed geographic clichés, tourist-brand logos, or text overlays.
```

## 5. Magazine opener spread

**Best for:** editorial concepting · **Format:** 2:1 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 2:1 magazine opening spread for an article about {{topic}}. Use one strong rights-safe hero image across
{{page_behavior}}, with the gutter protected. Add a restrained title block and deck on {{text_page_or_area}}.

TEXT — RENDER VERBATIM
"{{headline}}"
"{{deck}}"
"BY {{author_name}}"

No body copy, issue number, barcode, fake publication logo, invented photo credit, or decorative filler text.
Keep all faces and critical details away from the gutter; verify exact copy.
```
