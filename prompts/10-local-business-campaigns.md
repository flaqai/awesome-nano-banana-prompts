# Local Business Campaign Prompts

## 1. Restaurant weekly-special poster

**Best for:** cafes and restaurants · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 4:5 weekly-special poster for {{restaurant_type}}. Hero image: an honest, appetizing photograph of
{{actual_dish}} using only {{verified_ingredients}}. Mood: {{mood}}; surface: {{surface}}; light: natural side light.
Render exactly: "{{special_name}}" "{{availability}}" "{{price_and_currency}}" "{{short_CTA}}".

Do not invent toppings, discounts, delivery partners, awards, dietary claims, or contact details. Keep price prominent,
food realistic, and all copy readable on a phone. Verify currency and availability.
```

## 2. Fitness studio class campaign

**Best for:** gyms and independent trainers · **Format:** 9:16 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 9:16 story poster for {{class_name}} at {{studio_name}}. Show a diverse, rights-safe small group performing
{{realistic_movement}} with correct form in {{studio_environment}}. Natural athletic bodies, real sweat and fabric,
energetic but credible light. Render exactly: "{{class_name}}" "{{day_time}}" "{{level}}" "{{booking_CTA}}".

No body transformation claims, calories, medical benefits, fake urgency, logos, or impossible poses. Keep text clear of
faces and story UI zones. Verify schedule and level.
```

## 3. Real-estate listing hero

**Best for:** property marketing · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Image 1–{{n}}: current property references. Create a photorealistic 3:2 listing hero from the best supplied viewpoint.
Preserve architecture, room dimensions, window positions, finishes, view, and permanent fixtures. Improve only exposure,
white balance, straight verticals, and removable clutter as explicitly listed: {{approved_removals}}.

Do not enlarge rooms, replace materials, alter the view, add furniture, remove structural elements, create sunlight that
was not present, or add text. The result must remain a truthful representation of the property.
```

## 4. Neighborhood retail opening

**Best for:** shops, salons, studios · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Design a welcoming 4:5 grand-opening post for {{business_name}}, a {{business_type}} in {{neighborhood}}. Combine one
authentic storefront/detail image with an original graphic motif derived from {{local_non_protected_detail}}.
Render exactly: "NOW OPEN" "{{business_name}}" "{{opening_hours}}" "{{street_or_area}}".

No fake reviews, discounts, maps, logos, QR codes, phone numbers, or landmarks unless supplied. Avoid cultural clichés.
Verify address wording and hours character by character.
```

## 5. Regional tourism mini-guide

**Best for:** visitor bureaus and hosts · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 4:5 illustrated mini-guide titled "{{region}} IN ONE DAY" for {{audience}}. Show a morning, afternoon, and
evening sequence using only these verified places and activities: {{itinerary}}. Use geographically and seasonally
plausible architecture, landscape, transport, clothing, and food. Add times only when verified.

If grounding is enabled, check official opening information current to {{date}}. Do not promise availability, invent
businesses, compress impossible travel times, or add copyrighted mascots. Include "CHECK HOURS BEFORE VISITING".
```
