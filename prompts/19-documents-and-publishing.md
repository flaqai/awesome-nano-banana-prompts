# Documents and Publishing Prompts

## 1. White-paper cover and opening spread

**Best for:** research and B2B publishing · **Format:** A4 portrait plus 2-page spread · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a coordinated white-paper cover and opening spread for {{organization}}. Cover: one original visual metaphor,
title, subtitle, organization, and date. Opening spread: section title, 80–120 words of approved introduction,
one verified pull quote, and one supporting diagram. Copy: {{approved_copy}}. Use a disciplined grid, print-safe
margins, accessible typography, and a visual system derived from {{brand_rules}}.

No fake ISBN, barcode, author, citation, statistic, partner logo, or filler text. Protect the gutter and verify every
line, footnote, date, and page relationship.
```

## 2. Illustrated user-manual page

**Best for:** product onboarding · **Format:** A4 portrait · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create one A4 user-manual page explaining {{verified_task}} for {{product}}. Show exactly {{step_count}} numbered
steps with consistent product diagrams, hand positions, arrows, warnings, and required tools. Render only the approved
step text and safety language: {{approved_copy}}. Use large labels, high contrast, and a clear top-to-bottom sequence.

Do not invent operations, omit a safety guard, change product geometry, add a certification symbol, or paraphrase
warning text. Verify every hand interaction, arrow, part name, number, and warning hierarchy.
```

## 3. Encyclopedic reference plate

**Best for:** educational publishing · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 3:2 encyclopedic plate about {{subject}} for {{reader_level}}. Combine one hero illustration, {{detail_count}}
detail studies, a scale comparison, a compact taxonomy or parts list, and one contextual vignette. Use only the
verified labels and facts supplied in {{approved_data}}. Style: precise, calm, information-dense but readable.

Do not invent taxonomy, dimensions, dates, function, behavior, or citations. Keep labels connected to the correct
feature and distinguish illustrative scale from measured scale.
```

## 4. Annual-report data spread

**Best for:** organizational reporting · **Format:** 2:1 spread · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Design a two-page annual-report spread titled "{{section_title}}" using only {{verified_data}}. Include one primary
chart, two supporting metrics, one short approved narrative, and source/period notes. Use honest axes, consistent units,
accessible colors, clear page hierarchy, and a protected gutter. Distinguish actual, target, and estimate explicitly.

Do not invent performance, round values, hide a baseline, truncate an axis deceptively, add a testimonial, or create
decorative data. Verify every number, unit, date range, legend, and source line.
```

## 5. Workshop workbook page

**Best for:** courses and facilitated sessions · **Format:** A4 or US Letter · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a printable workbook page for {{workshop_topic}}. Include a short objective, one worked example, one reflection
prompt, a three-column exercise table, and a next-action box. Render exactly the approved instructional copy:
{{approved_copy}}. Use generous writing space, grayscale-friendly contrast, clear numbering, and accessible type.

No inspirational filler, fake citation, stock illustration, answer key, tiny text, or decorative background that uses
printer ink unnecessarily. Verify that all fields are writable and instructions fit on one page.
```
