# Animals, Creatures, and Botanical Prompts

## 1. Wildlife field-guide plate

**Best for:** education and conservation · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a scientifically responsible 3:2 field-guide plate for {{species_common_and_scientific_name}}.
Show one adult in a natural resting pose, a smaller habitat vignette, a scale silhouette, and exactly
{{detail_count}} labelled anatomical or identification details: {{verified_details}}. Medium: precise
natural-history illustration on warm neutral paper with restrained color and clear leader lines.

If grounding is available, verify markings and range with authoritative conservation sources. Do not invent
sexual dimorphism, range, behavior, or anatomy. No decorative script, logo, watermark, or unrelated species.
```

## 2. Identity-preserving pet portrait

**Best for:** personal commissions · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Images 1–3: the same pet from different views, supplied with owner permission. Create a photorealistic 4:5
portrait in {{simple_environment}}. Preserve species, breed traits, face shape, eye color, ear position,
muzzle markings, coat pattern, scars, age cues, body proportions, and collar exactly. Pose: {{natural_pose}};
camera: eye level with the animal; light: soft {{lighting}}.

Do not humanize expression, change markings, enlarge eyes, add costume, remove age signs, duplicate limbs,
or add text. Compare the final face and coat map against all references.
```

## 3. Original creature anatomy sheet

**Best for:** games and worldbuilding · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create an original biological concept sheet for {{creature_role_and_habitat}}. Show a full-body neutral view,
skeletal logic silhouette, locomotion sequence, feeding apparatus detail, footprint, scale beside a human,
and three adaptations tied to {{environmental_pressures}}. Design must obey a consistent number of limbs,
joints, mass distribution, breathing strategy, and surface material.

No existing franchise creature, random spikes, decorative anatomy, inconsistent limb count, or impossible center
of mass. Render only these labels: {{approved_labels}} and verify them across every view.
```

## 4. Botanical identification plate

**Best for:** horticulture and education · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 4:5 botanical plate for {{plant_common_and_scientific_name}}. Show whole growth habit, leaf front and back,
flower, fruit or seed when applicable, stem cross-section, and a scale bar. Use only verified morphology:
{{verified_features}}. Medium: layered watercolor with precise ink details and large readable labels.

Do not combine traits from related species, show simultaneous life stages without labelling them, invent medicinal
claims, or use decorative pseudo-Latin. Verify venation, arrangement, flower parts, labels, and scale.
```

## 5. Ecosystem relationship scene

**Best for:** classroom explainers · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 16:9 ecosystem explainer for {{ecosystem}} showing exactly these verified organisms and relationships:
{{organisms_and_relationships}}. Arrange the habitat as one coherent scene, then overlay restrained arrows and a
legend for producer, consumer, decomposer, pollinator, and habitat dependency where applicable.

Do not invent predation, place species outside their range, imply every relationship happens simultaneously, or
add unverified population claims. Use only approved labels and make every arrow direction scientifically meaningful.
```
