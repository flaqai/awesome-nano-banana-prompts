# Brand and Advertising Prompts

## 1. Multilingual product launch key visual

**Best for:** beverage, beauty, food, or consumer-goods launches  
**Format:** 4:5, 2K or 4K · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a premium 4:5 launch poster for the fictional product {{product_name}}.
Hero subject: {{product_description}}, occupying 60% of the frame on {{pedestal_or_surface}}.
Art direction: {{brand_mood}}, {{palette}}, realistic materials, controlled studio highlights,
and one distinctive visual motif: {{motif}}. Keep a clear top safe zone and mobile readability.

TEXT — RENDER VERBATIM
"{{brand_name}}"
"{{english_tagline}}"
"{{localized_tagline}}"

Use a clear visual hierarchy and optically balance both languages. Do not invent ingredients,
awards, certifications, prices, logos, or fine print. Preserve supplied package geometry and
label proportions. Verify every character and show one product only.
```

## 2. Outdoor billboard with instant readability

**Best for:** awareness campaigns · **Format:** 21:9 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Design a 21:9 roadside billboard for {{brand_or_campaign}}. Communicate one idea in under three
seconds: {{single_message}}. Use one large subject, a bold silhouette, strong day/night contrast,
and generous negative space. Preview the design as a flat front-facing billboard artwork, not a
street mockup.

Render exactly: "{{headline_under_six_words}}" and "{{short_CTA}}".
No other text. Keep all copy inside a 12% safe margin and large enough to read from a distance.
No QR code unless supplied. No invented logo; use Image 1 artwork exactly if provided.
```

## 3. Brand world moodboard

**Best for:** early creative direction · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a cohesive 16:9 brand-world moodboard for {{brand_concept}} aimed at {{audience}}.
Use a disciplined 3×3 grid containing: hero material, environment, product-use moment, palette,
typography mood, texture macro, packaging silhouette, human gesture, and lighting reference.
Visual personality: {{three_adjectives}}. Palette: {{colors}}. Materials: {{materials}}.

This is an original direction board, not a collage of recognizable campaigns. Do not include
third-party logos, celebrity faces, copied packaging, or named living-artist imitation. Use only
the text "{{brand_name}} — VISUAL DIRECTION". Make all nine cells feel like one system.
```

## 4. Seasonal retail campaign system

**Best for:** creating a master visual that crops well · **Format:** 1:1 master · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a square master campaign visual for {{season_or_event}} featuring {{product_or_service}}.
The central composition must survive crops to 4:5, 9:16, and 16:9. Keep the hero subject inside
the central 60%, with flexible atmosphere around it. Visual metaphor: {{original_metaphor}};
lighting: {{lighting}}; palette: {{palette}}; mood: {{mood}}.

Render exactly once: "{{campaign_line}}". No other copy. Avoid generic holiday clip art and
unlicensed cultural symbols. Preserve product identity and leave clean extension zones on every
edge. Before finalizing, mentally test all four crops for subject and copy safety.
```

## 5. Brand manifesto poster

**Best for:** internal launch, event wall, social statement · **Format:** 2:3 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Design an original 2:3 typographic manifesto poster for {{organization}}. Build a strong reading
sequence from one headline, three short principles, and one closing line. Combine expressive type
scale with {{abstract_material_or_scene}}, while keeping every word legible.

TEXT — RENDER VERBATIM
"{{headline}}"
"01 — {{principle_one}}"
"02 — {{principle_two}}"
"03 — {{principle_three}}"
"{{closing_line}}"

Do not paraphrase, repeat, hyphenate, or add filler copy. No third-party marks. Check numbering,
punctuation, and line order character by character.
```
