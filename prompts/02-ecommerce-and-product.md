# E-commerce and Product Prompts

## 1. Marketplace hero packshot

**Best for:** product listing main image · **Format:** 1:1 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Image 1–3: rights-cleared product references from front, side, and three-quarter views.
Create a clean square marketplace hero image of the exact product on a pure {{background_color}}
background. Preserve silhouette, dimensions, color, materials, seams, ports, closures, label art,
and all visible components. Center the product, fill 78–85% of the canvas, use a natural soft
ground shadow, accurate perspective, and crisp edges.

One product only. No props, hands, badges, text overlays, added accessories, reflections that hide
details, or redesigned labels. Compare geometry with all three references before finalizing.
```

## 2. Feature comparison board

**Best for:** listing image two or three · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 4:5 feature board for {{product}}. Show one accurate hero render plus three circular
detail crops connected with thin callout lines. Features and exact labels:
1. "{{feature_one}}" 2. "{{feature_two}}" 3. "{{feature_three}}".
Style: clean editorial commerce design, {{palette}}, large readable type, restrained iconography.

Use only claims supplied above. Do not invent test results, percentages, certifications, or
competitor comparisons. Keep callouts attached to the correct physical area. Preserve product
geometry and label. Verify each label and its leader line.
```

## 3. Exploded-view product story

**Best for:** technical marketing and crowdfunding · **Format:** 3:4 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Image 1: exterior product reference. Image 2: verified component diagram.
Create a refined 3:4 exploded-view visualization of {{product}} with exactly {{component_count}}
layers arranged along one clean axis. Use Image 2 only for verified component order and Image 1
for exterior identity. Render the shell slightly translucent where needed; use realistic materials,
consistent scale, soft studio light, and precise leader lines.

TEXT — RENDER VERBATIM
{{approved_component_labels}}

Do not invent internal parts or engineering specifications. Keep labels large and unambiguous.
If an internal relationship is not provided, omit it. Verify layer count and label mapping.
```

## 4. Lifestyle scene with product fidelity

**Best for:** product page storytelling · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Image 1–3: product identity references. Create a photorealistic 3:2 lifestyle image showing
{{target_user}} naturally using the exact product while {{action}} in {{environment}}. The product
must remain recognizable and unobstructed; match reference materials, color, scale, interface,
and label placement. Camera: {{camera_direction}}. Light: {{lighting}}. Mood: {{mood}}.

No staged pointing, floating product, impossible grip, extra fingers, altered branding, duplicate
product, or unrelated props. Keep skin and fabric texture natural. No text overlays.
```

## 5. Variant family lineup

**Best for:** colorways, flavors, or sizes · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Images 1–{{variant_count}}: one approved reference for each product variant.
Create a 16:9 family lineup with exactly {{variant_count}} products in the supplied order.
Maintain identical scale, camera angle, baseline, label proportions, and lighting while preserving
each variant's correct color and name. Arrange a gentle rhythm, with the hero variant slightly
forward but not larger. Background: {{background}}; lighting: clean shared studio setup.

Do not merge labels, exchange colors, duplicate variants, invent flavors, or alter packaging.
Render no text outside the real package artwork. Count and compare every variant before finalizing.
```
