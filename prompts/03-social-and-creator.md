# Social and Creator Content Prompts

## 1. YouTube thumbnail with one idea

**Best for:** explainers and creator videos · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Create a high-clarity 16:9 YouTube thumbnail about {{video_topic}} for {{audience}}.
Use one expressive focal subject, one supporting visual clue, and a simple background with strong
separation at phone size. Composition: {{subject_placement}}; emotion: {{emotion}}; palette:
{{high_contrast_palette}}. Render exactly one short headline: "{{two_to_five_word_hook}}".

No extra labels, fake platform UI, red arrows, circles, reaction clutter, logos, or misleading
visual claims unless explicitly requested. Keep face and text inside the central safe area.
```

## 2. Educational carousel cover and system

**Best for:** Instagram or LinkedIn carousel · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create slide 1 of a coherent 7-slide 4:5 carousel about {{topic}}. Establish a reusable system:
bold headline zone, one original visual metaphor, page number position, {{palette}}, and accessible
high-contrast typography. Cover text exactly: "{{headline}}" and "{{supporting_line}}" and "1/7".

Keep 15% safe margins and design for phone viewing. No body paragraphs, invented statistics,
third-party logos, or decorative microtext. The visual system must support later slides without
requiring a new composition style.
```

## 3. Podcast episode cover

**Best for:** episode artwork · **Format:** 1:1 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Image 1: approved host portrait. Create a square episode cover for {{podcast_name}}.
Preserve the host's identity, age, skin tone, hair, and expression. Integrate a visual metaphor for
{{episode_theme}} using {{medium_or_style}}, with a strong small-thumbnail silhouette.

TEXT — RENDER VERBATIM
"{{podcast_name}}"
"{{episode_title}}"
"EP. {{episode_number}}"

Do not invent a guest, sponsor, platform badge, or episode quote. Keep text away from the face and
verify spelling and number. No imitated celebrity likeness.
```

## 4. Event announcement post

**Best for:** workshops, meetups, openings · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Design a 4:5 event announcement for {{event_type}} using {{visual_concept}}. Make date and title the
first read, then venue and time. Render exactly:
"{{event_title}}"
"{{date}} · {{time}}"
"{{venue}}"
"{{CTA}}"

Use {{palette}} and {{typography_mood}}. Do not add sponsors, ticket prices, addresses, URLs, or QR
codes unless supplied. Check time-zone wording and every numeral. Keep copy inside mobile safe zones.
```

## 5. Quote card without fake attribution

**Best for:** founder or editorial social content · **Format:** 1:1 · **Difficulty:** Beginner

```text
Create a square editorial quote card with {{background_visual}} and a calm, readable hierarchy.
Render the quote exactly: "{{verified_quote}}". Attribution exactly: "— {{speaker_and_role}}".
Use opening and closing quotation marks once, preserve punctuation, and allow generous line spacing.

Do not alter the quote, fabricate credentials, add a portrait unless supplied, or add decorative
sentences. If attribution is uncertain, replace it with "SOURCE TO VERIFY" rather than guessing.
```
