# Infographics and Education Prompts

## 1. Process infographic

**Best for:** tutorials and onboarding · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 16:9 infographic explaining {{process}} to {{audience}}. Use exactly {{step_count}} numbered
stages in a left-to-right flow, one simple illustration per stage, consistent arrows, large labels,
and a final outcome panel. Title: "{{title}}". Step labels exactly: {{approved_step_labels}}.

Do not merge, reorder, rename, or invent steps. Avoid paragraphs and tiny text. Use {{palette}} with
accessible contrast. Verify numbering, arrows, and the connection between each label and illustration.
```

## 2. Scientific cross-section

**Best for:** classroom explainers · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a scientifically accurate 3:2 cross-section of {{subject}} for {{education_level}}.
Show only these structures: {{verified_structures}}. Use clean color separation, thin leader lines,
a scale cue when appropriate, and large labels. Title: "{{title}}".

Use authoritative terminology supplied here; if grounding is enabled, verify against {{primary_source}}.
Do not invent anatomy, imply false scale, or add decorative organs/components. If uncertain, omit the
detail and report the omission in accompanying text. Verify every leader line endpoint.
```

## 3. Historical timeline

**Best for:** museums, schools, editorial explainers · **Format:** 21:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create a 21:9 horizontal timeline of {{topic}} from {{start_date}} to {{end_date}} with exactly
{{event_count}} verified events. Use era-appropriate visual motifs without copying archival art.
For each event, render only: date, short title, and one-sentence caption from this approved data:
{{timeline_data}}.

Maintain chronological spacing and distinguish exact dates from approximate periods. Do not invent
quotes, portraits, flags, borders, or causal claims. Verify all dates with supplied primary sources.
```

## 4. Illustrated city guide map

**Best for:** tourism and neighborhood guides · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create an illustrated 4:5 orientation map of {{district_or_city}} for {{audience}}. Use a simplified,
not-to-navigation street network based on the supplied map reference. Show exactly {{landmarks}}
with numbered markers, a legend, north arrow, river/park distinctions, and title "{{map_title}}".
Art direction: {{original_style_properties}}.

Do not claim routing accuracy, invent businesses, alter landmark names, or use copyrighted mascots.
Label the map "ILLUSTRATED GUIDE — NOT FOR NAVIGATION". Verify marker-to-legend mapping.
```

## 5. Recipe learning card

**Best for:** culinary education and localized content · **Format:** 4:5 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 4:5 visual recipe card for {{dish}} aimed at {{skill_level}}. Use one ingredient grid and
exactly {{step_count}} illustrated cooking steps. Render only the approved title, quantities, timing,
temperature, allergen note, and steps: {{verified_recipe_data}}. Style: bright natural food
illustration with realistic ingredient colors and clear utensil shapes.

Do not modify quantities, cooking temperature, allergen information, or food-safety instructions.
Do not add health claims. Verify units, fractions, step order, and doneness cue.
```
