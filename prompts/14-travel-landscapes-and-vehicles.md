# Travel, Landscapes, and Vehicle Prompts

## 1. Weather-specific landscape editorial

**Best for:** destination and environmental stories · **Format:** 3:2 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape of {{specific_location_or_biome}} during {{season_and_weather}}.
Foreground: {{foreground_anchor}}; middle distance: {{human_scale_or_path}}; background: {{landform}}.
Use meteorologically plausible clouds, visibility, vegetation condition, water behavior, and light.
Camera: {{viewpoint}} with natural dynamic range and restrained color grade.

If a real place is named, verify distinctive geography when grounding is available. Do not combine unrelated
landmarks, exaggerate scale, add fantasy light, introduce people or buildings, or over-saturate the scene.
```

## 2. Responsible road-trip campaign

**Best for:** tourism and mobility marketing · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Create a 16:9 road-trip campaign image showing {{vehicle_type}} safely traveling through {{landscape}}.
Show correct lane position, road markings, guardrails, weather response, tire contact, and scale. Composition:
wide environmental view with the vehicle occupying less than 20% of the frame and open copy space at {{side}}.
Mood: {{mood}}; time: {{time}}; camera: realistic roadside tracking perspective.

No identifiable third-party badge, dangerous driving, road obstruction, impossible dust, fake location signs,
text, or environmental damage. Keep occupants unidentifiable unless rights-cleared references are supplied.
```

## 3. Vehicle orthographic concept board

**Best for:** original mobility concepts · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create an original 16:9 design board for {{vehicle_concept}} showing consistent front, rear, side, and three-quarter
views plus one interior detail. Design intent: {{intent}}. Package constraints: {{wheelbase_seating_cargo_or_use}}.
Materials and finish: {{materials}}. Use neutral studio lighting, aligned baselines, and identical design language
across every view. Render only these labels: {{approved_labels}}.

No existing manufacturer design, badge, impossible wheel steering, inconsistent doors, changing light signatures,
or invented performance specifications. Verify geometry continuity across all views.
```

## 4. Aerial geographic explainer

**Best for:** visitor orientation and environmental education · **Format:** 16:9 · **Difficulty:** Advanced

```text
Create an oblique aerial explainer of {{area}} using the supplied verified map and terrain references. Show only
{{landmarks_or_zones}}, with numbered markers and a legend. Preserve relative orientation, shoreline, major routes,
elevation logic, and north direction. Style: realistic terrain with simplified readable overlays.

Render "ILLUSTRATED OVERVIEW — NOT FOR NAVIGATION". Do not invent roads, trails, boundaries, businesses, travel
times, or emergency routes. Verify every marker and label against the supplied data.
```

## 5. Public-transit destination poster

**Best for:** city campaigns and events · **Format:** 2:3 · **Difficulty:** Intermediate

```text
Design an original 2:3 destination poster encouraging travel to {{destination}} by {{transit_mode}}. Use a bold,
rights-safe graphic composition based on {{local_visual_motif}}, with a clearly recognizable but factually accurate
vehicle silhouette. Render exactly: "{{headline}}" "{{route_or_station}}" "{{date_or_service_note}}".

No operator logo unless supplied, no fake timetable, fare, route claim, landmark mash-up, vintage-poster copy,
or extra text. Verify destination spelling, route notation, and vehicle details.
```
